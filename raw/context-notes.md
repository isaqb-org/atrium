# Context notes

Collected while discussing vision and goals, 2026-11-23.

- Training providers maintain their training dates in DIWAN, not in atrium.
  atrium takes the dates over through an interface.
- atrium replaces today's www.isaqb.org.
- Provider profiles may move to DIWAN (open). If so, the vision's list of
  access points loses "provider profiles".
- Today only providers know their training participants, and only
  certification bodies know who is certified. With DIWAN, providers report
  the participants of every training, so iSAQB gets a complete alumni
  register for trainings from DIWAN's launch on. Earlier participants are
  missing.
