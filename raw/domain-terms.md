## Notes

- **training**: aliases training course, course, accredited training. A
  provider's accredited course for one curriculum. The training is
  accredited.
- **training date**: aliases date, session, course date. One scheduled run
  of a training. The training date is scheduled and booked.
- **training format**: aliases delivery format, delivery mode. How a
  training date is delivered: classroom, online, eLearning, or hybrid.
  Hybrid is atrium's own addition, not an iSAQB format. Never say bare
  "format": distinguish from data format (JSON, ICS) of the feeds.
- **DIWAN**: alias DIWAN register. iSAQB's central register of all
  Foundation Level and Advanced module training participants; issues
  tamper-proof digital certificates of attendance. Also the providers'
  self-service for training dates. Accredited providers must register every
  training and its participants in DIWAN, from DIWAN's launch on. How atrium
  talks to DIWAN belongs to context and an external interface, not here.
- **content contributor**: aliases content provider (former name), author.
  A role, not a stakeholder: whoever supplies editorial content to atrium
  and maintains it. Holders: the iSAQB GmbH through the editorial team
  (news, marketing material), the association's working groups (curricula,
  explanations), authors (blog posts, news). Binding content goes through
  an independent review first. "Provider" alone always means training
  provider. Distinguish from reviewer, who checks and approves.
