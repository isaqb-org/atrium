# Stakeholder names

English names for the stakeholders of the earlier isaqb-atrium wiki, agreed
one by one on 2026-11-24. Aliases are English. The German translation is an
optional extra, kept apart from the aliases.

| Name | Aliases | German (optional) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| training provider | provider, accredited training provider | Trainingsanbieter | Accredited organisation or individual that offers CPSA trainings. |
| trainer | accredited trainer | Trainer:in | Person accredited per level or module who delivers CPSA trainings for a training provider. |
| member | iSAQB member, association member | Mitglied | Natural or legal person belonging to the iSAQB association. The member types are part of the domain term, not separate stakeholders. |
| certification prospect | prospect | Interessierte | Anyone aiming for any CPSA level (Foundation, Advanced, Expert). Roles overlap: an alumnus can be a prospect too. |
| employer | company, HR department | Arbeitgeber | Organisation that qualifies its staff in software architecture: books in-house trainings, plans certification for a team, checks certificates. Sponsoring members are a member type; providers are training providers. |
| alumni | alumnus, alumna, alum, former participant | Alumni | People who attended at least one accredited training, whether or not they took an exam. Being certified is a different thing. |
| editorial team | editors, content team | Redaktion | Maintains atrium's editorial content: texts, news, and translations (commissioning and checking them). Possibly reviewer of provider profiles, if those stay in atrium. Currently the iSAQB GmbH. |
| operator | operations, ops | Betreiber | Role that runs atrium in production: deployment, hosting, monitoring, backups, updates. Who holds it is open. |
| iSAQB Infrastructure Working Group | IS-WG | Infrastruktur-Arbeitsgruppe | Working group that runs iSAQB's infrastructure besides atrium; atrium has to fit into it. Possible holder of the operator role. |
| external IT support | IT service provider | externer IT-Support | External service provider for IT support, under contract. Vested interest in simple, reliable operations, so technical and architecture decisions matter to it. Possible holder of the operator role. Named as a role only, vendor-neutral. |
| executive board | iSAQB board, board | Vorstand | Executive body of the iSAQB association; sets goals for atrium and decides their open points. Not the same as the English website's "Active Board Members", who are active personal members. |
| product owner | PO | Product Owner | Elicits, clarifies and decides atrium's requirements and interfaces, in agreement with the stakeholder groups. Recommended as a small group of 2–3 people from the association; not yet adopted by the executive board. |
| technical owner | TO, architect | Technical Owner | Makes architecture and implementation decisions for atrium, its interfaces, operations and infrastructure; may delegate, with its own budget. Decides how atrium is run; the operator carries it out. Recommended holder: the iSAQB CTO; not yet adopted. |
