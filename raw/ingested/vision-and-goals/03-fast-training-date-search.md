# Objective: find a fitting training date fast

Parent: excellent usability.

- **Purpose.** Anyone looking for a training finds a fitting date fast, across
  all accredited providers. A date is fitting if it matches the visitor's
  module, location, language, format and period.
- **Advantage.** Prospects get to a training without detours, and providers
  receive bookings.
- **Metric.** Success rate and median time for the task "find a fitting
  date", measured in the usability test of the core tasks.
- **Target.** Success rate ≥ 90 %, median time under 60 seconds.
- **Horizon.** At launch.

How the search offers the criteria (filters, free text, map) is left to the
functional requirements. Provider neutrality is covered by the fairness
objective.
