# Objective: clear path to certification

Parent: excellent usability.

- **Purpose.** Prospects quickly understand the path to CPSA certification
  (Foundation Level, Advanced Level, training, exam at a certification body)
  and know their next step.
- **Advantage.** Fewer questions about certification, more prospects start
  the certification path, more transparency about iSAQB's work and
  curricula, and iSAQB-authored content is easy to find.
- **Metric.** Share of test persons who correctly state their next step after
  at most 5 minutes on the site, measured in the usability test of the core
  tasks.
- **Target.** ≥ 80 %.
- **Horizon.** At launch.

## Test personas

| Starting point | Example of a next step |
|---|---|
| New to CPSA | Foundation Level training, or self-study plus exam |
| Holds CPSA-F, wants to go further | Choose Advanced Level modules, collect credit points |
| Has the Advanced credit points | Register for the Advanced Level exam |

## Open questions

- Who defines the correct next step per persona before the test?
- Transparency about iSAQB's work and findable iSAQB-authored content reach
  beyond certification. They may become an objective of their own.
