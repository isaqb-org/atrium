# Objective: multilingual platform

Parent: vision.

- **Purpose.** atrium is available in the languages of the international
  CPSA audience, and adding a language is translation work only.
- **Advantage.** The international audience finds content in its language,
  and iSAQB adds languages without investing in technology.
- **Metric.** (1) Languages in which atrium's own text is completely
  available. (2) Per new language: did it need a change to code, build or
  hosting (yes/no)?
- **Target.** German and English at launch. Spanish and French within
  6 months after launch. No new language needs a change to code, build or
  hosting. Italian, Dutch and Chinese are candidates without a date.
- **Horizon.** Launch, and 6 months after launch.

## Definitions and limits

- **Completely available** covers atrium's own text: navigation, UI,
  explanatory pages (certification path, FAQ, glossary) and error messages.
  Content written by others (curricula, provider data, articles) appears in
  the languages it exists in, with a visible note when it is missing in the
  page language.
- Left-to-right scripts only for now. Right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hebrew)
  are out of scope.

Ordering providers or trainers by page language is covered by the fairness
objective.
