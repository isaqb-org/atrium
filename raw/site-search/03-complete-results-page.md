# Story: complete results on a results page of its own

Parent: site search. Priority: must.

**As** a visitor **I want** to see all results on a page of their own and
pass on its address, **so that** I can browse them, open the search again
later or share it.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- Given the popup is open and no result is selected, when I press Enter or
  choose "Show all results", then the results page opens with all results.
- Given the results page, then it groups the results by category and lets
  me narrow them to one or more categories.
- Given the address of the results page, when someone opens it, then they
  see the same query and category choice, with the results as of now.
- Given no result matches, then the page says so, repeats the query, and
  offers the closest terms and the main entry points.
