# Story: find by synonym

Parent: site search. Priority: should.

**As** a visitor **I want** to find a term by another name too, **so that**
I need not know what iSAQB calls it.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- Given the public glossary lists synonyms of a term ("provider" for
  training provider), when I search for a synonym, then the term is among
  the first 3 results.

The synonyms are a column in the public glossary.
