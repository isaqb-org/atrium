# Story: use the finder's filters on small screens

Parent: find an accredited training, step 3. Priority: must.

**As** a certification prospect **I want** to use the training finder on my
phone as well as on a desktop, **so that** I can find a date on the go.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- Given a screen narrower than 600 px, when I open the finder, then the
  filters are collapsed; hit count and set filters stay visible.
- Given a reference smartphone, when I carry out "find an accredited
  training", then I succeed as often as on a desktop.
