# Vision

atrium is the online platform of iSAQB. It gives everyone who wants to reach
iSAQB online one clear way in to certification, the training market and the
association's work. atrium covers all of iSAQB's online access points: the
public website, provider profiles, contact and newsletter, and data feeds.

Scope of this vision: purpose and scope only. Qualities (provider neutrality,
usability, multilingualism, cost) are separate objectives. Stakeholders are
not listed here.

Origin: short version of GOAL-001 from the earlier isaqb-atrium wiki, agreed
2026-11-23.
