# Objective: low cost of ownership

Parent: vision.

- **Purpose.** atrium is cheap to host and operate.
- **Advantage.** The association spends less money on technology, and the
  platform stays maintainable with volunteers and a small budget.
- **Metric.** Annual cost of hosting and operation: services (hosting,
  domains, CDN, newsletter, monitoring, backups) and operational work (fixes,
  updates). Development of new features is excluded.
- **Target.** Below €1,000 per year.
- **Horizon.** From launch, every year.

Today's cost is known to the association and deliberately not recorded here.

Origin: short version of GOAL-008 from the earlier isaqb-atrium wiki, agreed
2026-11-23.
