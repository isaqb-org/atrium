# iSAQB

**Definition.** Umbrella name for two legally separate organisations: the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]] and the [[DT-003-isaqb-gmbh|iSAQB GmbH]].

**In context.** Without a qualifier, "iSAQB" in curricula, exam rules and on the website usually means the association. In everyday use it often means the GmbH as well.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]] and [[DT-003-isaqb-gmbh|iSAQB GmbH]]. Where the difference matters, for example in the imprint, the privacy notice or contracts with providers, atrium says which of the two is meant.
