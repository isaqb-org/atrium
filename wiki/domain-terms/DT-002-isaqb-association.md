# iSAQB association

**Definition.** The non-profit registered association iSAQB e. V., owner of the [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] programme and origin of all [[DT-012-curriculum|curricula]], rules and [[DT-013-accreditation|accreditations]].

**In context.** Its [[DT-004-member|members]] decide in the [[DT-005-general-meeting|general meeting]]. The [[DT-003-isaqb-gmbh|iSAQB GmbH]] runs operations on its behalf.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-003-isaqb-gmbh|iSAQB GmbH]]: the association decides on content, the GmbH runs operations. [[DT-001-isaqb|iSAQB]]: the umbrella name for both.
