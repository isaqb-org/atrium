# iSAQB GmbH

**Definition.** The [[DT-002-isaqb-association|association]]'s non-profit operating company, founded 2018, which handles administration, business operations and marketing.

**In context.** It currently provides atrium's editorial team.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]]: decides on content and owns the [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] programme. [[DT-001-isaqb|iSAQB]]: the umbrella name for both.
