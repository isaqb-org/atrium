# member

**Definition.** A natural or legal person belonging to the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]].

**In context.** The statute knows personal members (active or passive), supporting members and honorary members. Only active personal members vote. All members together form the [[DT-005-general-meeting|general meeting]]. The English labels of the member types differ between the statute and the website.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-018-alumni|Alumni]] attended a training, certified persons passed an exam. Neither makes someone a member. The groups overlap but are not the same.
