# general meeting

**Definition.** The assembly of all [[DT-004-member|members]] of the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]], responsible for everything the statute does not assign to another body.

**In context.** It elects the executive board.

**Distinguish from.** Executive board: represents the association and is elected by the general meeting.
