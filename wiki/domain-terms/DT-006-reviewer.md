# reviewer

**Definition.** A role, not a stakeholder: the person who checks provider profiles and binding editorial content on behalf of iSAQB and releases them for publication.

**In context.** The [[STK-007-editorial-team|editorial team]] is one possible holder of the role. [[DT-020-training-date|Training dates]] are reviewed in [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]], not by the reviewer. If provider profiles move to DIWAN, the reviewer checks editorial content only.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-033-content-contributor|Content contributor]]: supplies content, the reviewer checks and approves it. The review of a [[DT-019-training|training]] during [[DT-013-accreditation|accreditation]] checks training material, not profiles.
