# CPSA

**Definition.** Certified Professional for Software Architecture: the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|association]]'s modular, international training and certification programme, and the title held after passing its exam.

**In context.** Three levels: [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]] (CPSA-F), [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]] (CPSA-A) and [[DT-010-expert-level|Expert Level]] (CPSA-E).

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-001-isaqb|iSAQB]]: the organisation behind the programme.
