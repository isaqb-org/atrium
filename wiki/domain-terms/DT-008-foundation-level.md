# Foundation Level

**Definition.** CPSA-F, the entry level of the [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] programme, ending in a multiple-choice exam at a [[DT-014-certification-body|certification body]].

**In context.** Aimed at people with development experience who want to take on the role of software architect. More than 18 months of practical experience is recommended. A training is strongly recommended but not required for the exam.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]]: the second level, modular, with a written assignment instead of multiple choice.
