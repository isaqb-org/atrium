# Advanced Level

**Definition.** CPSA-A, the second level of the [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] programme: candidates combine [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level modules]] to earn credit points, then pass a written assignment and an oral defence.

**In context.** Exam prerequisites: a passed [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]], professional experience and at least 70 credit points from accredited trainings, at least 10 in each of the three competence areas, proven by [[DT-015-certificate-of-attendance|certificates of attendance]]. The exam is not based on a [[DT-012-curriculum|curriculum]]; its rules are set out in the exam rules that iSAQB publishes on its website, which atrium does not specify.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]]: the entry level, multiple choice. [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]]: one building block of the Advanced Level.
