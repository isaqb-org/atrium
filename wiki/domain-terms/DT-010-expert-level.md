# Expert Level

**Definition.** CPSA-E, the third and highest level of the [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] programme: candidates work in a moderated topic group and are assessed by two independent examiners.

**In context.** Requires a passed [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]] and several years of experience as an architect. It has its own [[DT-012-curriculum|curriculum]] but no accredited [[DT-019-training|trainings]] and no [[DT-020-training-date|training dates]].

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]]: written assignment and oral defence, the prerequisite for the Expert Level.
