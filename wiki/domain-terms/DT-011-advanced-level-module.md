# Advanced Level module

**Definition.** A self-contained [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]] topic with its own [[DT-012-curriculum|curriculum]] and a fixed number of credit points per competence area.

**In context.** Attending an accredited [[DT-019-training|training]] for a module earns its credit points toward the Advanced Level.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]]: the level as a whole. [[DT-019-training|Training]]: one provider's course for a module. [[DT-032-module-page|Module page]]: atrium's page about a module.
