# curriculum

**Definition.** The document published by iSAQB that fixes learning goals, their exam relevance and minimum duration for the [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]], an [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]] or the [[DT-010-expert-level|Expert Level]].

**In context.** [[DT-016-training-provider|Training providers]] may use a curriculum only under a usage licence.

**Distinguish from.** Training material: duration, didactics, exercises, examples and structure of a [[DT-019-training|training]] are designed by the training provider. A curriculum is not a complete description of software architecture.
