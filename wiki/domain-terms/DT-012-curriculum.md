# curriculum

**Definition.** The document published by iSAQB that fixes learning goals and minimum duration for the [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]] or an [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]]. Only the Foundation Level exam is based on a curriculum, which also fixes the learning goals' exam relevance. The [[DT-010-expert-level|Expert Level]] has none.

**In context.** [[DT-016-training-provider|Training providers]] may use a curriculum only under a usage licence. A curriculum comes in versions. The Foundation Level exam is taken against the current version; after a new Foundation Level curriculum is published, a grace period of a few weeks still allows the previous one. [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]] certification follows other rules, set out in the exam rules that iSAQB publishes on its website; atrium does not specify them. The upcoming, current and previous version each have a [[DT-035-curriculum-page|curriculum page]] with the download.

**Distinguish from.** Training material: duration, didactics, exercises, examples and structure of a [[DT-019-training|training]] are designed by the training provider. A curriculum is not a complete description of software architecture. Exam rules (German: Prüfungsordnung): how certification works, in particular at the Advanced Level; not a curriculum.
