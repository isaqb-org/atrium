# accreditation

**Definition.** iSAQB's formal confirmation that a [[DT-016-training-provider|training provider]], a [[DT-017-trainer|trainer]] or a [[DT-019-training|training]] meets its requirements, the precondition for offering CPSA trainings.

**In context.** A training is accredited through a one-time review of its training material and then carries the seal "iSAQB Accredited Training".

**Distinguish from.** Licensing applies to [[DT-014-certification-body|certification bodies]], although some iSAQB material also calls them "accredited". Certification applies to persons who passed an exam and is not an accreditation.
