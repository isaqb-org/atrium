# certification body

**Definition.** An organisation licensed by iSAQB and independent of [[DT-016-training-provider|training providers]] that conducts [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] exams and issues certificates.

**In context.** iSAQB separates training from examination: whoever trains does not examine. For the [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]], the provider or trainer usually chooses the certification body. For the [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]], the participants choose.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-016-training-provider|Training provider]]: trains, does not examine. The exam rules speak of "licensed" certification bodies, some iSAQB material of "accredited" ones, see [[DT-013-accreditation|accreditation]].
