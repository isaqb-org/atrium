# certificate of attendance

**Definition.** A [[DT-016-training-provider|training provider]]'s confirmation that a person attended an accredited [[DT-019-training|training]], stating the credit points earned, issued via [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]].

**In context.** For the [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]] exam, candidates submit their certificates of attendance to the [[DT-014-certification-body|certification body]]. For [[DT-018-alumni|alumni]], it proves their status.

**Distinguish from.** CPSA certificate: issued by a certification body after a passed exam. The certificate of attendance proves attendance only.
