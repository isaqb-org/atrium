# training provider

**Definition.** An organisation or individual accredited by iSAQB to offer and deliver [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] [[DT-019-training|trainings]].

**In context.** A training provider commits to deliver trainings according to the [[DT-012-curriculum|curricula]] and to use only accredited [[DT-017-trainer|trainers]]. Each training and each trainer needs its own [[DT-013-accreditation|accreditation]].

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-014-certification-body|Certification body]]: whoever trains does not examine. "Provider" alone always means training provider, never exam provider or [[DT-033-content-contributor|content contributor]]. [[DT-017-trainer|Trainer]]: the person who delivers the training.
