# trainer

**Definition.** A person accredited by iSAQB for a specific level or module, entitled to deliver CPSA [[DT-019-training|trainings]] for or with a [[DT-016-training-provider|training provider]].

**In context.** Trainers are named on [[DT-020-training-date|training dates]]. Trainer names are personal data.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-016-training-provider|Training provider]]: the organisation that offers the training.
