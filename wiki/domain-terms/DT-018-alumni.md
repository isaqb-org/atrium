# alumni

**Definition.** People who have attended at least one iSAQB-accredited training, whether or not they later took an exam.

**In context.** The [[DT-015-certificate-of-attendance|certificate of attendance]] proves the status. With [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]], iSAQB has a register of participants for trainings from DIWAN's launch on.

**Distinguish from.** Certified persons passed an exam, [[DT-004-member|members]] belong to the association. The groups overlap: whoever takes the [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]] without a training is certified but not an alumnus, and whoever attends a training without an exam is an alumnus but not certified.
