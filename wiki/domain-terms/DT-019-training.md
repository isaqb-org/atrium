# training

**Definition.** A [[DT-016-training-provider|training provider]]'s accredited course that teaches one iSAQB [[DT-012-curriculum|curriculum]] ([[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]] or one [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]]), delivered by accredited [[DT-017-trainer|trainers]].

**In context.** The training is what gets accredited, through a one-time review of its material. Trainings may differ in duration, didactics, exercises and structure.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-020-training-date|Training date]]: one scheduled run of a training. The training is accredited, the training date is scheduled and booked.
