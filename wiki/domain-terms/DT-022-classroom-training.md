# classroom training

**Definition.** iSAQB's [[DT-021-training-format|training format]] for a training delivered in person, at scheduled times, at a fixed location.

**In context.** A training date in this format always has a location (city and country).

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-023-online-training|Online training]]: scheduled, but without a location. [[DT-024-elearning|eLearning]]: neither fixed location nor fixed time. [[DT-025-hybrid-training|Hybrid training]]: a location plus online participation.
