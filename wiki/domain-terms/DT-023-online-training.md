# online training

**Definition.** iSAQB's [[DT-021-training-format|training format]] for a virtual classroom held at scheduled times, attended from any location.

**In context.** A training date in this format has no location but fixed times, so it needs a time zone.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-022-classroom-training|Classroom training]]: a fixed location. [[DT-025-hybrid-training|Hybrid training]]: online is one of two ways to attend. [[DT-024-elearning|eLearning]]: no fixed times.
