# eLearning

**Definition.** iSAQB's [[DT-021-training-format|training format]] for flexible, self-paced learning with videos and digital material, sometimes complemented by virtual sessions with a tutor.

**In context.** A training date in this format has neither location nor fixed time, only an access or availability period. iSAQB spells it "eLearning".

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-023-online-training|Online training]]: fixed times in a virtual classroom. Tutor sessions do not turn an eLearning into an online training.
