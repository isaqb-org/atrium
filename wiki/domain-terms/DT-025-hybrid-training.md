# hybrid training

**Definition.** atrium's fourth [[DT-021-training-format|training format]], not defined by iSAQB: a scheduled training that some participants attend in the classroom and the rest online at the same time, with the same trainer.

**In context.** A training date in this format has a location and a time zone.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-022-classroom-training|Classroom training]]: in person only. [[DT-023-online-training|Online training]]: online only. atrium has no "blended learning" format.
