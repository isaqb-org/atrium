# DIWAN

**Definition.** iSAQB's central register of all [[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]] and [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]] training participants, which issues tamper-proof digital [[DT-015-certificate-of-attendance|certificates of attendance]].

**In context.** Accredited [[DT-016-training-provider|training providers]] must register every training and its participants in DIWAN, from DIWAN's launch on. DIWAN is also the providers' self-service for their [[DT-020-training-date|training dates]]. What the name DIWAN stands for is open.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-027-atrium|atrium]]: DIWAN is not part of atrium.
