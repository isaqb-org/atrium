# atrium

**Definition.** The umbrella name for all of iSAQB's online access points: the public website, provider profiles, contact and newsletter, and data feeds.

**In context.** The name comes from architecture: an atrium is the open courtyard at the centre of a building, with rooms around it. The courtyard stands for iSAQB, the rooms for the access points.

**Distinguish from.** Website (www.isaqb.org): only one of the access points. [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]]: not part of atrium.
