# training finder

**Definition.** The public [[DT-027-atrium|atrium]] view where people find the published [[DT-020-training-date|training dates]] of all accredited providers, with filters.

**In context.** Usable without login. Shows only training dates delivered by [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]]. Each provider has a [[DT-030-provider-page|provider page]].

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-029-admin-app|Admin app]]: not public, behind a login. [[DT-036-site-search|Site search]]: the search over all public content, which hands over to the finder for training dates.
