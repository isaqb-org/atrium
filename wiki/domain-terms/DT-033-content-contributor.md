# content contributor

**Definition.** A role, not a stakeholder: whoever supplies editorial content to [[DT-027-atrium|atrium]] and maintains it.

**In context.** Holders: the [[DT-003-isaqb-gmbh|iSAQB GmbH]] through the editorial team (news, marketing material), the association's working groups (curricula, explanations) and authors (blog posts, news). Binding content goes through an independent review first.

**Distinguish from.** [[DT-016-training-provider|Training provider]]: "provider" alone always means training provider. [[DT-006-reviewer|Reviewer]]: checks and approves, does not supply.
