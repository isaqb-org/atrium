# partner logo marquee

**Definition.** The moving row of logos of all active accredited [[DT-016-training-provider|training providers]] on the [[DT-027-atrium|atrium]] start page, in an order drawn at random on every visit.

**In context.** One logo per provider, each linked to its [[DT-030-provider-page|provider page]]. The first display that [[GOAL-009-fairness-towards-providers-and-trainers|fairness]] applies to. Requirements: [[FR-010-show-the-partner-logo-marquee|FR-010]].

**Distinguish from.** Provider list: the alphabetical list of providers, for example in the finder's filter, which may stay sorted. [[DT-028-training-finder|Training finder]]: sorted by start date.
