# Find a term by synonym

## Story

As a **visitor** I want **to find a term by another name too**, so that **I need not know what iSAQB calls it**.

**Acceptance criteria.** > follows [[acceptance-criteria]]

- **Given** the [[DT-031-public-glossary|public glossary]] lists synonyms of a term ("provider" for training provider) **when** I search for a synonym **then** the term is among the first 3 results.

The synonyms are a column in the public glossary.
