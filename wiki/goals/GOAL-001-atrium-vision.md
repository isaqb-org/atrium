# atrium vision

## Vision

For everyone who wants to reach iSAQB online, **[[DT-027-atrium|atrium]]** is iSAQB's online platform that gives **one clear way in to [[DT-007-cpsa|certification]], the training market and the association's work**. It covers all of iSAQB's online access points: the public website, provider profiles, contact and newsletter, and data feeds.

Purpose and scope only. The qualities are objectives of their own.
