# Excellent usability for every audience

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]], [[SMART]] (target and horizon set)

- **Purpose.** Every audience does its core tasks on atrium fast and without help.
- **Advantage.** Fewer drop-outs, fewer questions to the iSAQB office.
- **Metric.** SUS score after a usability test of the core tasks, see `metric:`, `target:`, `horizon:`.

**Core tasks.** The usability test and the sub-goals refer to this list.

| Audience | Core task |
|---|---|
| [[STK-004-certification-prospect\|Certification prospect]] | Understand the path to certification |
| [[STK-004-certification-prospect\|Certification prospect]] | Choose a suitable module |
| [[STK-004-certification-prospect\|Certification prospect]] | Find an accredited training |
| Everyone | Look up a term in the [[DT-031-public-glossary\|public glossary]] |

**Sub-goals.** [[GOAL-003-fast-training-date-search]], [[GOAL-004-clear-path-to-certification]].

**Parent.** see `parent:`.
