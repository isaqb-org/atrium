# Find a fitting training date fast

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]], [[SMART]] (target and horizon set)

- **Purpose.** Anyone looking for a [[DT-019-training|training]] finds a fitting [[DT-020-training-date|training date]] fast, across all accredited providers. A date is fitting if it matches the visitor's module, location, language, [[DT-021-training-format|training format]] and period.
- **Advantage.** Prospects get to a training without detours, and providers receive bookings.
- **Metric.** Success rate and median time, see `metric:`, `target:`, `horizon:`.

How the search offers the criteria (filters, free text, map) is left to the functional requirements. Provider neutrality is covered by [[GOAL-009-fairness-towards-providers-and-trainers]].
