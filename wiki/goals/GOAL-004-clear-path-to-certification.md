# Clear path to certification

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]], [[SMART]] (target and horizon set)

- **Purpose.** Prospects quickly understand the path to [[DT-007-cpsa|CPSA]] certification ([[DT-008-foundation-level|Foundation Level]], [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]], training, exam at a [[DT-014-certification-body|certification body]]) and know their next step.
- **Advantage.** Fewer questions about certification, more prospects start the certification path, more transparency about iSAQB's work and [[DT-012-curriculum|curricula]], and iSAQB-authored content is easy to find.
- **Metric.** Share of test persons who name their correct next step, see `metric:`, `target:`, `horizon:`.

**Test personas.**

| Starting point | Example of a next step |
|---|---|
| New to CPSA | Foundation Level training, or self-study plus exam |
| Holds CPSA-F, wants to go further | Choose [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level modules]], collect credit points |
| Has the Advanced credit points | Register for the Advanced Level exam |

**Parent.** see `parent:`.
