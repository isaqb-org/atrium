# Multilingual platform

## Objective   > follows [[PAM]], [[SMART]] (target and horizon set)

- **Purpose.** atrium is available in the languages of the international CPSA audience, and adding a language is translation work only.
- **Advantage.** The international audience finds content in its language, and iSAQB adds languages without investing in technology.
- **Metric.** Completely available languages, see `metric:`, `target:`, `horizon:`. Italian, Dutch and Chinese are candidates without a date.

**Completely available** covers atrium's own text: navigation, UI, explanatory pages (certification path, FAQ, [[DT-031-public-glossary|glossary]]) and error messages. Content written by others ([[DT-012-curriculum|curricula]], provider data, articles) appears in the languages it exists in, with a visible note when it is missing in the page language.

**Limits.** Left-to-right scripts only for now. Right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hebrew) are out of scope. Ordering providers or trainers by page language is ruled out by [[GOAL-009-fairness-towards-providers-and-trainers]].
