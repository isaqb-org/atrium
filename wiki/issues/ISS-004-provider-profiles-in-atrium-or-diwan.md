**Affects.** [[DT-029-admin-app|admin app]], [[DT-030-provider-page|provider page]], [[DT-006-reviewer|reviewer]], [[DT-027-atrium|atrium]]

**Context / evidence.** Open since the vision discussion on 2026-11-23.

**Options.**
1. Profiles stay in atrium: atrium keeps the admin app and the profile review.
2. Profiles move to DIWAN: the admin app goes away, atrium only shows profiles.
