# Credit point needs a domain term

**What's unresolved.** Four definitions use "credit point" without defining it.

**Affects.** [[DT-009-advanced-level|Advanced Level]], [[DT-011-advanced-level-module|Advanced Level module]], [[DT-015-certificate-of-attendance|certificate of attendance]], [[DT-032-module-page|module page]]

**Context / evidence.** Proposed definition: a unit an accredited Advanced Level training earns per competence area. The Advanced Level exam requires at least 70, with at least 10 in each of the three competence areas.

**Options.**
1. Add the domain term with the proposed definition.
2. Leave it undefined.
