# Which English labels of the member types are authoritative?

**What's unresolved.** The statute and the English website label the member types differently, and "supporting member" means different things in each.

**Affects.** [[DT-004-member|member]] (domain term), [[STK-003-member|member]] (stakeholder), and the [[STK-011-executive-board|executive board]] page, which distinguishes itself from the website's "Active Board Members".

**Context / evidence.** Probable mapping, unconfirmed:

| Statute (DE) | Literal EN | English website, probably |
|---|---|---|
| aktives persönliches Mitglied | active personal member | Active Board Members |
| passives persönliches Mitglied | passive personal member | Supporting Members |
| förderndes Mitglied | supporting member | Sponsoring Members |
| Ehrenmitglied | honorary member | (none) |

**Options.**
1. The statute's literal translations are canonical, the website labels become aliases.
2. The website labels are canonical, the statute's terms become aliases.
