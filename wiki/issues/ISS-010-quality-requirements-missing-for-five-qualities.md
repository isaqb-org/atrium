# Quality requirements missing for security, modularity, extensibility, flexibility and maintainability by volunteers

**What's unresolved.** [[GOAL-006-technical-excellence-of-the-running-site]] measures the running site with Lighthouse and axe only. Five qualities have no measurable scenario: security, modularity, extensibility, flexibility, and maintainability by volunteers (from [[GOAL-008-low-cost-of-ownership]]).

**Affects.** [[GOAL-006-technical-excellence-of-the-running-site]], [[GOAL-008-low-cost-of-ownership]]

**Context / evidence.** Example scenario for the last one: "a volunteer updates all dependencies in under two hours".

**Options.**
1. One quality requirement per quality, each with a stimulus, response and measure.
2. An ISO 25010 quality model for atrium, with GOAL-006 measured against it.
