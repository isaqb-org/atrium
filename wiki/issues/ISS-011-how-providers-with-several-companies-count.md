# Does a provider with several companies count once or once per company?

**What's unresolved.** The fairness metric of [[GOAL-009-fairness-towards-providers-and-trainers]] compares each provider's share of top positions with k/n. Some [[STK-001-training-provider|training providers]] operate through several legal companies. The human chose on 2026-11-25 to count each accredited company once, provisionally, and asked the [[STK-011-executive-board|executive board]] to decide.

**Affects.** [[GOAL-009-fairness-towards-providers-and-trainers]]

**Context / evidence.** The answer depends on whether accreditation contracts are made per company or per group.

**Options.**
1. Once per accredited company (current, provisional): a group with three companies gets three times the visibility.
2. Once per provider: a group gets the same visibility as a one-person provider.
