# Does the public wiki site need pages of its own, beyond tagline, about page and brand?

**What's unresolved.** Whether the tagline, the about page and the brand are enough for the showcase, or whether the site needs pages the dashboard does not have, such as a landing page with its own story.

**Affects.** [[DR-001-publish-the-whole-wiki-as-a-public-static-site|DR-001]]. The answer decides whether the proposed rule holds that an instance customizes the dashboard only through configuration, brand and about page, never through its own templates or pages; that rule keeps dashboard updates painless.

**Context / evidence.** Open question of the discussion note (SRC-020).

**Options.**
1. Configuration, brand and about page are enough.
2. atrium adds a landing page of its own, and the dashboard needs a way to carry it through updates.

**Resolution.** Option 1, decided by the human on 2026-11-26: tagline, about page and brand are enough. The about page tells the showcase story. The config-only rule holds and is recorded in [[DR-001-publish-the-whole-wiki-as-a-public-static-site|DR-001]].
