# executive board

**Snapshot.** Represents the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]] and is elected by the [[DT-005-general-meeting|general meeting]]. Sets goals for atrium and decides their open points. Not the same as the English website's "Active Board Members", who are active personal [[DT-004-member|members]].

**Goals.**
- The association visibly stays neutral towards providers and trainers.

**Concerns / pains.**
- A silent preference for single providers that nobody decided, for example through a fixed order.
