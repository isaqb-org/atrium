# product owner

**Snapshot.** Decides atrium's goals, functional and quality requirements, main content areas, navigation and the interfaces to [[DT-026-diwan|DIWAN]] and similar systems, in agreement with the stakeholder groups. Works closely with the [[STK-013-technical-owner|technical owner]].

**Goals.**
- Clear, agreed requirements, decided by exactly one body.

**Concerns / pains.**
- Coordination with all main stakeholder groups: [[STK-001-training-provider|training providers]], the association, the GmbH, working groups, [[STK-002-trainer|trainers]] and the [[STK-013-technical-owner|technical owner]].
- At least in the start phase, the work goes beyond what volunteers can do.

**Interactions.**

> [!note] Recommended, not adopted
> Recommended as a small group of 2 to 3 people from the association. Not yet adopted by the [[STK-011-executive-board|executive board]].

> [!note] Estimates
> Influence and interest are estimates, not confirmed by a human.
