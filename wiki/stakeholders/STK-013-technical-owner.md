# technical owner

**Snapshot.** Makes the architecture and implementation decisions for atrium, its interfaces, operations and infrastructure. Needs sound knowledge of the existing infrastructure and works closely with the [[STK-012-product-owner|product owner]]. Decides how atrium is run, and the [[STK-008-operator|operator]] carries it out.

**Goals.**
- A sustainable architecture and smooth operations.

**Concerns / pains.**
- May and should delegate implementation and support, and needs its own budget for it.
- Designs layouts for special content, such as campaigns, together with the product owner and [[DT-033-content-contributor|content contributors]].
- Shapes operations: monitoring, backup, maintenance, availability, SLAs.

**Interactions.**

> [!note] Recommended, not adopted
> Recommended holder: the iSAQB CTO. Not yet adopted by the [[STK-011-executive-board|executive board]].
