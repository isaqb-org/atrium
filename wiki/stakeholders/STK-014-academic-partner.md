# academic partner

**Snapshot.** A university or university of applied sciences that teaches a CPSA curriculum or holds CPSA exams, with iSAQB's support. Supporting accreditation and examination at universities is one of the tasks of the [[DT-002-isaqb-association|iSAQB association]]. Academic partners appear in atrium separately from [[STK-001-training-provider|training providers]]: not in the training finder, on provider pages or in the logo marquee. Their courses are part of a degree programme, not bookable trainings. Whether they maintain a profile in atrium depends on [[ISS-004-provider-profiles-in-atrium-or-diwan]].
